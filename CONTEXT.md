# Frontier Research

This file defines only the language used by the active Codex research path.

## Goal

**NorthStar**: The currently approved end state. It remains fixed until the
user replaces it explicitly.

**Success Condition**: A real-world outcome that determines whether the goal
was reached. Internal biology can explain or constrain it but does not replace
it.

**Whole-Body Human Capability Frontier**: The current approved outcome in
`goal.json`: develop skeletal structure, whole-body physiology and physical
capabilities together at 110–120 kg toward their jointly attainable human
frontier by maximizing the equal-weight arithmetic mean of normalized metrics.
Candidate body configurations are compared through model-supported tradeoffs
and synergies, using the same metric set and normalization for each candidate.
Metric definitions and model connections remain to be established; a search's
best model-bounded result is not proof of an absolute human maximum.
Skeletal length, geometry and microstructure are research variables.
No fixed 24-month deadline or inherited numerical targets govern this goal.
Boxing and future MMA remain applications, not the sole success definition.

**Reverse Design**: Reason backward from the desired whole-body outcome and
score to the structures, physiology, mechanisms, and feasible methods needed
to produce it; test those proposals forward through evidence and qualified
models. Move freely between broad synthesis and fine detail, and across systems,
as findings create questions. This is revisable scientific reasoning, not a
fixed sequence or a search over invented biological relationships.
The supplied numerical research targets are in `docs/TARGETS.md`; normalized
comparison rules and the uncomputed result table are in `docs/SCORECARD.md`.

**Combat Capability Frontier**: A historical goal and current application: integrated
ability to attack, defend, pressure, control distance and contact, decide, and
sustain effective output against a resisting opponent at the human limit. The
seven Physical Success Targets and 27 Quantitative Subtargets remain historical
reference concepts, not required targets of the current goal.

The current goal develops whole-body capacities; combat technique remains a
parallel interest and application.
The causal meaning of combat capability is itself investigated from scientific
evidence, primary combat observations and joint discussion with the user.
Examples of questions are not mandatory questions. The approved goal is fixed;
Codex's explanation and proposed strategy remain revisable.
The body supplies capacity; technique expresses it against an opponent. Each
can change what the other needs. Existing mass, horizon, and selected technical
reference conditions remain explicit.

**Physical Success Target**: A whole-capacity outcome. Historical examples are
force production, explosiveness, repeated combat output, movement control,
structural durability, fatigue resistance and recovery, or sensorimotor
performance. It contains functional components and requires combat-task
transfer; no single test establishes it.

**Quantitative Subtarget**: A numerical measurement anchor linked
beneath one or more Physical Success Targets. It preserves a useful frontier
boundary but does not substitute for the parent target or integrated combat
performance.

**Current State**: The smallest useful description of the user's present
condition, observations, constraints, and relevant uncertainty. It may contain
ordinary user reports; a formal measurement contract is not required. Its
absence does not block research or reference-boundary simulation from human
evidence, physical laws, and validated models; later observations refine
personalization.

**Promising Method**: A possible route toward the goal that research currently
supports enough to keep considering. It is not an activated strategy.

## Research

### Cultivation language (user-facing aliases)

The user wants sustained cultivation: developing body, capabilities, understanding
and methods together, not merely a finished report or a gamified workout plan.
Xianxia supplies inspiration for questions, not evidence of biological mechanisms.
These aliases do not rename tool fields, change NorthStar or introduce workflow states.

| Expression | Research meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 수련의 길 | The user's continuing pursuit of development; not a promise of endless gains. |
| 목표 법체 | The approved desired whole-body configuration, with its actual metrics unchanged. |
| 수련 현황 | Current State, including observation dates and uncertainty. |
| 공법 / 공법 연구·개량 | A method and its research or revision; a proposal is not activated practice. |
| 근골과 육체 기반 | Structural support such as bone, muscle and tendon; not a fixed innate rank. |
| 수련의 병목 | A suspected constraint to investigate, not an alternative weakest-link objective. |
| 깨달음 | A revised insight; identify whether it is evidence-supported or still a hypothesis. |
| 돌파 / 경지 다지기 | A demonstrated new capability / its repeatability and retention, not arbitrary levels. |
| 수련록 | Research and practice records, distinguishing actual practice from proposed or simulated work. |
| 도반 | The collaborative relationship between user and research agent, not scientific authority. |

Retain literal scientific terms for hypotheses, experiments, simulation and
uncertainty. Do not equate fictional 단전, 경맥 or 혈맥 with real organs or genes.
Ask how observed change alters the next useful method or opens new capabilities
when relevant, without turning these examples into mandatory questions or stages.

**Hypothesis**: A tentative explanation or possible improvement that Codex may
create, revise, combine, or discard naturally during research. “Frontier” is
not a separate artifact class.

**Evidence**: A retained external source or user document with enough
provenance to inspect again.

**Evidence Passage**: A short exact excerpt plus a locator and the one retained
claim it supports. It grounds a claim in inspected content without treating a
search-result record as read evidence.

**Quality Appraisal**: Codex's explicit, source-bounded description of study
type, population, methods, results, directness, and important bias or quality
limits. The storage layer validates its representation but does not generate or
score the appraisal. It may be concise free text or a structured assessment; the code
does not decide whether the source is scientifically persuasive.

**External Model**: An established scientific model or engine used inside its
documented context. It includes physics-based and mechanistic models such as
multibody dynamics, finite-element, fluid, physiological, tissue, cellular, and
metabolic models. Its output constrains reasoning but does not by itself prove a
whole-person or personal outcome.

**Model Layer**: A non-persistent routing label connecting a model's documented
scale to compatible physical targets. It prevents one scale from standing in
for unrelated whole-person, organ, musculoskeletal, tissue, or cellular work;
it does not choose hypotheses or models for Codex.

**Experiment Record**: An immutable record of an external-model question,
exact input, version, provenance, raw output, and optional Codex interpretation.

**Personal Observation**: An immutable numerical observation with time, goal
links, unit, protocol, instrument, uncertainty when known, and provenance.
Repeated same-protocol observations permit descriptive comparison; they do not
identify causality or prove a personal response.

**Traceable Research Statement**: A concise hypothesis, note, or promising
method labeled as external-evidence-supported, user-observed, model-bounded,
measurement-derived, inferred, hypothetical, or unknown, with the receipts
required by strong factual labels. Exploratory hypotheses, inferences, and
unknowns may remain plain language.

**Goal Research Map**: A passive linked view from the fixed whole-person goal
through combat tasks and performance capacities to possible diagnostic
systems, intervention families, and observations. The links route research;
they do not add success conditions, prove personal causality, choose a
strategy, or activate an intervention.

**Research Note**: A concise retained finding, failed route worth avoiding,
important unknown, or decision-relevant lesson.

**Current Understanding**: Codex's revisable, free-text explanation of the goal,
evidence, strategy, and unresolved questions. It has no required outline and
does not control research. Replacing it preserves earlier versions; retained
notes and observations are not erased.

**Simulation Laboratory**: The installed external models and their short usage
guide. Codex creates each experiment's inputs and code when needed, uses the
engines directly, and retains useful files. There is no separate simulator service,
shared calculation interface, experiment registry or prescribed file template.

**Research Entry**: An optional immutable user or Codex contribution, with free
text and reply links. Saving a question, interpretation or plan draft confers
neither evidential support nor real-world approval.
Material human and parallel-agent contributions carry session/agent provenance
when available; attribution is descriptive, not an authentication mechanism.

**Research Run**: An opt-in operational record for one runner-owned native Codex
goal: connection ownership, events, explicit resource limits and user controls.
It is not a scientific workflow, agenda, completion authority or activated strategy.

The boxing technical goal is researched from primary fight and training
footage. It does not require an unrelated physiological simulation.

## Removed concepts

Legacy scientific Harness runs, Planner/Reviewer decisions, mandatory measurement contracts,
fixed-target calculators, coverage audits, repository-invented biological or
longitudinal models, Frontier Candidate gates, Research Protocol lineage, fixed
question scores, and fixed 24-month screens are not part of the active
environment or its retained research data.
